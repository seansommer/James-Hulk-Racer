# Jamesy identity revision — v1.1

## Current decision

The owner approved the five-costume lineup shown in the conversation, then requested that **J replace the age-based 4 on the chest** so the character remains consistent over time. This revision is the current identity specification. Read it together with the original [ART_BIBLE.md](ART_BIBLE.md); it supersedes that document wherever an age numeral is specified as a costume emblem. Animation frame counts, world indices and other technical numbers are not changed.

## Emblem standard

- All five Jamesy costumes use one bold, upright, white capital **J** on the chest. Preserve each costume's existing chest-shield shape, colors, trim and proportions.
- The belt keeps its existing white **J**. It does not become an age number, a duplicate chest shield or a different logo.
- The Captain-style circular shield uses **J** in place of its age numeral as well, maintaining the same age-independent identity across the lineup.
- Do not print an age numeral elsewhere on a costume, shield, portrait, app badge or other new character-brand artwork. Do not add a J to the back of the cape simply to expose the chest mark from the racing camera.
- Lettering must never be mirrored. Rear views naturally hide the chest emblem; three-quarter views show only the physically visible portion.
- The original supplied images remain historical references. Their old chest and shield numerals are superseded; no new artwork should copy them.

## Preserve the approved character

Keep the approved friendly child face, large blue eyes, swept orange-red hair, warm skin, open eye mask, compact body proportions and polished storybook-arcade materials. Preserve the five approved costume palettes and silhouettes. The age-number change is not permission to age Jamesy up, redesign his face, replace the costumes or introduce new player accounts.

The lineup is approved as a visual reference, subject to this emblem revision. Individual turnaround views and animation frames are not automatically approved by that decision.

## Next art batch: Hulk five-pose identity proof

Create one clearly separated review board of the SAME green-and-purple Hulk-style Jamesy:

| View | Review goal |
| --- | --- |
| Front three-quarter, relaxed hero stance | Confirm the new chest J, belt J, face, hair, glove scale and costume trim. |
| Straight rear, neutral grounded stance | Establish cape exterior/lining, back-of-hair silhouette, shoulders and boot scale for racing. |
| Rear steering lean left | Show a readable whole-body bank without changing limb lengths or reflecting the image. |
| Rear steering lean right | Draw the opposite bank separately; preserve the same hair sweep and asymmetric details. |
| Rear three-quarter Smash contact | Show bent knees, both green padded fists at ground contact, clear silhouette and cape lifting away from the hands. |

Use a quiet light studio background, generous separation, a consistent scale and a shared grounded foot baseline where the pose permits. Keep every figure fully within its panel. Lighting stays soft from upper left. The purple cape with green lining/trim must agree between front and rear views. No scenery, large aura, lightning or ground explosion should hide the costume during this proof.

A pose review board is NOT a transparent sprite atlas or a finished run animation. Ground shadows on the board are for presentation; game shadows and contact effects will be separate layers.

After the five-pose proof is accepted, produce the existing eight-frame rear-run test at the planned 12 fps, with stable foot anchors, alternating contacts and a clean wrap from the last frame to the first. Verify it in motion before expanding to the full 64-frame Hulk package or the other four heroes.
